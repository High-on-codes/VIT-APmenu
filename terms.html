VIT AP Menu shows the VIT-AP hostel mess menu. It is an independent student project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by VIT-AP University or its mess contractors. By using the app you agree to these terms, alongside Apple's Standard End User Licence Agreement.

2Menu accuracy

Menus are entered from the mess's published schedule and can change at short notice. We try to keep them accurate but can't guarantee that what's shown is what's served. Meal times in the app are the usual ones and may differ on some days.

Allergies and dietary needs: the app doesn't list ingredients or allergens, and dish names alone aren't a reliable guide. If you have an allergy or dietary restriction, always check with the mess staff before eating.
