Frequently asked

Why does the menu look wrong / out of date?
The menu is maintained by the hostel mess coordinator and updated month to month. If something looks off, email the address above and it'll get checked.

Do I need to sign in?
No. Viewing the menu never requires an account. Sign-in only exists for the one admin who updates the menu.

What are "Week A" and "Week B"?
VIT-AP's mess menu repeats on a two-week cycle rather than a plain weekly one — each weekday has two different menus that alternate. The app shows which one applies automatically; tap the info icon next to the badge in the app for details.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?
Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for "VIT AP Menu", and add it.

Meal reminders aren't showing up.
Check Settings → Meal reminders is turned on, and that Notifications are allowed for the app in iOS Settings.
