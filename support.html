Frequently asked

The menu looks wrong or out of date.

Menus come from the mess's monthly schedule, and the mess can change dishes at short notice. First check that the right hostel block and mess plan are picked in Settings. If it still looks off, email us and it'll be checked.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Everything works without an account. Sign-in only exists for mess staff who update the menu.

What are “Week A” and “Week B”?

VIT-AP's mess menu repeats on a two-week cycle, so each weekday has two menus that alternate. The app works out which one applies from the date. Tap the Week badge in the app for details.

How do I use Siri?

Open the app once, then say “Hey Siri, what's for lunch at the mess?” or “What's for dinner in VIT AP Menu?”. You can say “mess” or “campus” instead of the app name. The shortcuts are also in the Shortcuts app.

How do I add a widget?

Home Screen: long-press an empty area, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for “VIT AP Menu”.

Lock Screen: long-press the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a VIT AP Menu widget.

How do I star a favourite dish?

Long-press any dish on the Today or Week tab and choose Add to Favourites, or type a word like “Paneer” in Settings. You'll get a notification 30 minutes before a meal that has it.

How do I stop the Lock Screen countdown?

Tap the ✕ on it, long-press the Dynamic Island and tap Stop, or tap the countdown button on the Today tab.

Notifications aren't showing up.

Check the toggles in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for VIT AP Menu in iOS Settings → Notifications.
