Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version: you don't need an account, and we don't track you. No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. Almost everything the app knows about you stays on your iPhone. The only thing that ever leaves it is feedback you choose to send, and that is anonymous.

This policy covers the VIT AP Menu app for iPhone (and its widgets, Lock Screen countdown and Siri shortcuts) and explains what data it handles, where, and why. VIT AP Menu is an independent student project and is not affiliated with VIT-AP University.

1. Information stored only on your device

These stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us:

  • Your chosen hostel block and mess plan (Regular or Special)
  • Your favourite dishes and alert settings
  • App settings, such as meal reminders and the auto-start countdown
  • A saved copy of the menu, so widgets, Siri and the app work offline
  • Small counters used to decide when to show the feedback prompt (the date you first opened the app and how many times you've opened it)

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

2. Feedback you choose to send

The app occasionally asks how it's working for you. If you tap Send, we receive:

  • Your star rating (1 to 5) and any comment you type
  • Your hostel block and mess plan
  • The app version and the time it was sent

We do not receive your name, email, Apple ID, phone number, device identifier or location, and the feedback isn't linked to you. Please don't include personal details in your comment. If you tap Skip for now, nothing is sent. Feedback is used only to improve the app and is readable only by the developer.

3. Menu and announcements

The app downloads the mess menu and any announcements from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). This is public, read-only content that contains no information about you. To deliver it, Google's servers process standard technical information such as your IP address, as any internet service does. We don't use this to identify you.

4. Staff sign-in

Students never sign in. A hidden sign-in exists only for mess staff who update the menu. It uses Firebase Authentication (email and password). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us or stored in the app.

5. Notifications, Siri, widgets and the Lock Screen countdown

  • Notifications (meal reminders and favourite-dish alerts) are scheduled entirely on your device. No push server is involved.
  • Siri and Shortcuts answers come from the menu saved on your device. Your voice request itself is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Widgets and the Lock Screen countdown read the menu saved on your device.

6. Ratings and sharing

"Rate on the App Store" and "Share with Friends" open Apple's App Store or the iOS share sheet. Anything you do there is governed by Apple's policies or the app you share to.

7. What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

8. Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) to store the menu, announcements and anonymous feedback. Google's handling of data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Feedback is kept for as long as it's useful for improving the app, and is deleted when it no longer is. Because feedback is anonymous, we can't look up "your" feedback, but if you want something you sent removed, email us the text and the approximate date and we'll delete it.

10. Children

The app is made for university students. It isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to ask about, correct or delete data about you. Since the app doesn't collect identifiable personal data from students, there's usually nothing to look up, but you can always contact us.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
