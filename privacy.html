The short version

  • No account needed, and no sign-up ever.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.
  • Your hostel, favourites and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • The only thing that leaves your phone is feedback you choose to send, and it's anonymous.

1What stays on your iPhone

DataWhere
Your hostel block and mess planOn device
Favourite dishes and alert settingsOn device
App settings (reminders, countdown)On device
A saved copy of the menu, for offline use, widgets and SiriOn device
When you first opened the app and how often, to time the feedback promptOn device
Feedback you choose to sendSent, anonymous

None of the on-device data is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

2Feedback you choose to send

The app occasionally asks how it's working for you. If you tap Send, we receive:

  • Your star rating (1 to 5) and any comment you type
  • Your hostel block and mess plan
  • The app version and the time it was sent

We do not receive your name, email, Apple ID, phone number, device identifier or location, and feedback isn't linked to you. Please don't put personal details in your comment. If you tap Skip for now, nothing is sent. Feedback is used only to improve the app and only the developer can read it.

3Menu and announcements

The app downloads the mess menu and any announcements from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). This is public, read-only content with no information about you. To deliver it, Google's servers process standard technical information such as your IP address, like any internet service. We don't use this to identify you.

4Staff sign-in

Students never sign in. A hidden sign-in exists only for mess staff who update the menu, using Firebase Authentication (email and password). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and are never visible to us or stored in the app.

5Notifications, Siri, widgets and countdown

  • Notifications (meal reminders and favourite-dish alerts) are scheduled entirely on your device. No push server is involved.
  • Siri and Shortcuts answers come from the menu saved on your device. Your voice request is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Widgets and the Lock Screen countdown read the menu saved on your device.

6Ratings and sharing

“Rate on the App Store” and “Share with Friends” open the App Store or the iOS share sheet. Anything you do there is governed by Apple's policies or the app you share to.

7What we never do

  • Show ads or use analytics or tracking
  • Sell or share data with anyone
  • Access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

8Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) to store the menu, announcements and anonymous feedback. See the Firebase privacy information.

9Keeping and deleting data

Feedback is kept while it's useful for improving the app, then deleted. Because it's anonymous we can't look up “your” feedback, but if you want something removed, email us the text and roughly when you sent it and we'll delete it.

10Children

The app is made for university students. It isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to ask about, correct or delete data about you. The app doesn't collect identifiable personal data from students, so there's usually nothing to look up, but you can always contact us.

12Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.

13Contact

Questions or requests: Support.ProjectZ@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use.
